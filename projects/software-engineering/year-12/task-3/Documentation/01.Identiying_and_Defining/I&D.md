# Identifying and Defining:

1. Define the problem it is being created to solve.

    In modern society, the idea of business cards has grown outdated. In the digital age, business cards create unnecessary waste as they are often discarded and simply unused. They are also constrained in the amount of information they can convey, due to their size. However, the concept of networking remains strong. This creates a delicate balance between being able to connect with others as well as provide information about yourself to others.

    Creating physical business can also lead to wasted financial resources, especially for small businesses or entrepreneurs. This means that the business will have to sacrifice resources and time instead of doing something more worthwhile. As the world grows more conscious of issues such as global warming, the waste of resources can creates rifts between business and consumer which can leads to damages to reputations and henceforth affect the business negatively.

2. What is the software you have decided to create?

    The software I have decided to create is a website that will serve as a online business card. Additionally, to replace the paper physical component, an NFC card loaded with the website will serve that purpose. On the website, users will be able to customise their own profile, filling it with contact information such as phone number, email, and social media. This means the website will be able to provide significantly more information compared to the average business card, while being more environmental friendly and is more financially efficient. All information from the user will be stored securely in the backend, using encryption and hashing to ensure that data security remains high.

    The physical business card will work using NFC (Near-Field Communication), which allows data transfer in a short distance (typically 4cm). NFC is used largely in contactless payments, such as Google and Apply Pay, operating at 13.56 MHz. By tapping the card on the back of a potential contact's phone, the business card will transfer the website URL or an offline version of the website to the phone, allowing the user to look directly at the contact details of the business owner and save them directly to the phone. In the event that the phone does not support NFC, a QR code can be printed onto the card as well as some basic information displayed on the card to ensure that it does not go to waste. Instead of printing hundreds upon hundreds of paper business cards and giving them out, business cards will remain with the business owner, cutting harshly down on wasted resources. This means significantly less cards will need to be printed, meaning the business can rely more on the web solution to network.

3. Define its scope/boundaries - what it will and will not do.

    The website ultimately aims to provide users with a/the client's contact information. The client will be able to edit their own personal page, so it can be populated with their contact details such as phone number, email, social media etc. It is not solely based for a single client, instead users can create their personal pages with their contact information. The contact details will be linked directly to their specific app/website. For example, an Instagram username, when clicked on, will open directly to Instagram.

    The client will have some basic control over the formatting, such as which social media details they want to display. However the overall structure will be defined by myself. The client will be able to set their profile to either private or public, depending on their preference. Users, when coming to the website, will have the option to get all the client's details emailed to them. This further opens networking opportunities. Users will also be able to search for public profiles if they desire. This will be done through a home page (index.html) which will be the default page if the user does not go directly to the client's profile.

    The web solution will not be able to create the external contact details of the client. It will be the client's responsibility to provide the links, usernames etc, for the web solution to display in a central location.

4. Who is it for? How would it be used? What are the important characteristics and/or features?

    This web solution is aimed mainly at entrepreneurs and small/growing business owners. It can also be used for students, or people fresh to the workforce. The web solution will serve as a central platform for users to share personal details and get in contact with each other. Essentially, this will be a virtual business card. Users will have customisation over what contact details they want to display publicly. The web solution will be paired with a physical NFC card which will replace traditional business cards, turning them digital. The website can be placed onto the NFC card, so that they can use NFC technology in mobile devices to share their details.

    The web solution will serve as a central hub empowering networking.

5. What constraints may affect the project?

    One of the constraints that may affect the project is how secure backend will be. The backend needs to be extremely secure as the web solution will be handling lots of personal information from users, hence making the need for personal privacy high. A lot of research and planning will need to be done to ensure this.

    My own personal knowledge on programming may be a constraint due to a lack of. I will need to ensure that my knowledge is keeping up with my ideas, and not to go too big in case I cannot achieve it.

    The client's needs may also affect the project, as they may change over time. I will need to work closely with the client to ensure all their needs are met and compromises made if I cannot.

6. What assumptions are being made about the scenario and/or users?

    It will be assumed that users know their own contact details, as the website will not create it for them. Additionally, users will need to have basic tech literacy, given that they will need to interact with the website to setup their profile and view other user's profiles. However, given that this project is aimed at a younger generation that has grown up with technology, the risk of users being unable to navigate the website is low.
